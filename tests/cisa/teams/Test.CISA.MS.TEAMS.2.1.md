Unrestricted federation lets users communicate with external organizations and increases exposure to phishing and data disclosure. This check passes when external access is disabled or restricted to at least one approved domain.

#### Remediation action

1. In the [Teams admin center](https://admin.teams.microsoft.com), go to **External collaboration** > **External access** > **Organization settings**.
2. Select **Allow only specific external domains**.
3. Add the approved domains and remove any domains that are not authorized.
4. Save and confirm the organization-wide change.
