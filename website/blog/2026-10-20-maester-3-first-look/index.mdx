I joined Rich Dean on the very first episode of Syncro's **Behind the Tenant** show, and it turned out to be one of the first public looks at [Maester 3.0](/blog/maester-3-0).

We talk about why Maester exists (starting with a Conditional Access policy that looked right in the portal but protected no one for a year), and then walk through what's new in 3.0: the new test engine, drift tracking between runs, tests that skip themselves when the tenant isn't licensed, framework mappings, and the affected objects view. We also cover MaesterCloud for MSPs who manage many tenants, and community Packs.

If the episode inspires you to write your own checks, try the brand new [Test Builder](pathname:///test-builder): pick the attributes of your test in a form, see the rule for each one, and get the code for your test as you go.
