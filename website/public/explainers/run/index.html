Maester merges your config files into one: what to run, settings for each check, and where results go.

Your selection picks checks by product, framework, severity, test ID or pack. -DryRun shows the list before anything runs.

A check that needs a service you didn't connect, or a licence you don't have, is skipped with a reason code. It is never marked failed.

The engine runs each planned check, enforces time-outs and turns a family into one row per policy, mailbox or app.

One result becomes the HTML report, JSON, JUnit for your pipeline, and email or Teams alerts.
